Privacy Policy

Effective 15 September 2026 Applies to Jonah on Android and iOS
In short

Jonah is a browser with an AI assistant built in. Your bookmarks and history stay on your device. When you use the assistant, Trust Engine, or Lens, the specific thing you asked about — a message, a page's domain, an image — is sent to our server to generate a response. We don't sell your data or run ads in Jonah.

  • On-device: bookmarks, browsing history, saved settings, subscription status
  • Sent to our server: chat messages, page text you ask about, domains checked by Trust Engine, media submitted to Lens
  • Handled entirely by Google: your payment details for subscriptions — we never see them

1 Information we collect

What Jonah collects depends entirely on which features you use. We built the app so that most of what makes it useful — your bookmarks, your history, your settings — never has to leave your phone.

Data collected by feature
FeatureWhat's collectedWhere it goes
Browsing Bookmarks, history, ad-block preference On-device only
Assistant chat Your messages, the page content you ask about Our server
Chat attachments Text extracted from a photo or file you attach Our server
Trust Engine The domain name of the site you're visiting Our server, public sources
Lens (media search) The image or video frames you submit Our server, Google
Voice commands Your spoken audio, transcribed on-device Your device's speech recognizer
Google account picker (Android only) Names of Google accounts already on your device Read locally, never sent
Subscriptions Purchase and entitlement status Google Play / Apple App Store

On Android, we never see your Google account password or its stored data — the account picker only reads the list of names already signed in on your device, so you don't have to retype an email address. This feature doesn't exist on iOS.

2 Processed only on your device

A few features do real work locally and send nothing anywhere unless you then choose to share the result in a chat message:

  • Text recognition and image labeling on photos you attach — this runs on-device (Apple's Vision framework on iOS, Google's ML Kit on Android), not a cloud service.
  • Reading PDF and text file attachments — extracted on your device before you decide whether to send it.
  • Scene detection in videos submitted to Lens — we pick out a handful of representative frames on your device rather than uploading the whole file.

3 How we use information

We use what you send us for exactly one purpose: answering the thing you asked. Chat messages and page content go to our AI model to generate a reply. A domain you're visiting goes into Trust Engine's scoring so it can tell you whether a site looks trustworthy. An image goes into Lens's search so it can tell you where else that image appears online.

We don't build advertising profiles, we don't sell data to third parties, and Jonah has no ad SDK or analytics tracker built in.

4 Services we rely on

Some features work by calling other services on your behalf. Here's exactly which ones, and what each one sees:

  • Our own backend (noahai.live) — receives chat messages, Trust Engine domain checks, and Lens media to generate responses.
  • Our search proxy (jonahbrowser.store) — forwards search and news queries to Google's and NewsAPI's services without exposing our API keys to your device.
  • Reddit's public search — Trust Engine reads publicly available Reddit posts about a brand or domain; no Reddit account is involved.
  • Google Play Billing (Android) / Apple's App Store (iOS) — handles your subscription purchase entirely; we receive only a confirmation of which plan you're on.

5 How long we keep it

Bookmarks and history live on your device until you clear them yourself, in Settings. Chat and Trust Engine requests are processed to generate a response and are not linked to your identity beyond a temporary session identifier used to keep a conversation coherent. Uninstalling Jonah removes everything stored on your device, including your usage-plan status — a fresh install starts over on the free tier until you sign back in to restore a purchase.

6 Your choices

  • Clear your browsing history any time from Settings → Data.
  • Deny the microphone or (on Android) Google-account permission at the OS level — Jonah still works as a browser without them, just without voice commands or the account picker.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all on-device data. To also stop future backend processing, simply stop using the affected features before uninstalling.

7 Children's privacy

Jonah is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we'll remove it.

8 Security

Data sent to our backend travels over encrypted HTTPS connections. Subscription and usage-plan data stored on your device is kept in your device's encrypted storage — the Keychain on iOS, encrypted local storage on Android — not in plain text. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we don't store anything we don't need to answer your request.

9 Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we'll update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of Jonah after a change means you accept the updated policy.

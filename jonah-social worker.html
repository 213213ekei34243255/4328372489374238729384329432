Our mission

Humanitarian work, guided by intelligence and compassion.

Jonah – CogniAI Initiative exists to put help where it is needed most: a roof for a family that has lost everything, a safe way out for people in danger, and a school meal and a classroom for a child in Africa.

CogniAI brings intelligent, data-informed planning to humanitarian work, helping us prioritise urgent needs and cut the delays between a gift and the people it serves. Technology supports the work — but it is people, trust and partnership that deliver it.

Shelter

Safe, dignified housing for Palestinian families who have lost their homes.

Safety

Emergency transport that moves civilians, the sick and the wounded out of danger.

Learning & nourishment

Education and food so African communities can grow strong and stay in school.

Our programs

Where your gift goes.

Two regions, four commitments. Every donation supports practical, life-changing help for people who need it most.

Palestine

Homes for Palestinian families

Families who have lost their homes need more than a tent. We fund safe, dignified shelter so people can live with security and privacy while they rebuild their lives.

  • Emergency and temporary shelter
  • Repair and rebuilding of damaged homes
  • Household essentials for families starting again
Fund a home

Safe & emergency transport

When staying is no longer safe, moving must be. We fund emergency transport that helps civilians — the wounded, the sick, children and the elderly — reach safety and care.

  • Emergency evacuation of civilians
  • Ambulance and medical transport
  • Safe routes planned through humanitarian channels
Fund safe passage

African communities

Education

Education is the surest path out of poverty. We support African communities with the learning materials and classroom support that keep children in school and learning.

  • School supplies and learning materials
  • Support for classrooms and teachers
  • Helping girls and boys stay in school
Fund a classroom

Food & nourishment

No child learns on an empty stomach. We fund food for families and communities facing hunger, so children can grow, learn and look ahead with hope.

  • Emergency food for families
  • School meals for children
  • Support for community food security
Fund meals
Our partnership

We work closely with the United Nations.

Humanitarian response is strongest when it is coordinated. We work closely with the United Nations and its humanitarian agencies so that help reaches people through trusted, established channels — avoiding duplication, responding to assessed needs, and keeping aid safe and accountable.

Our work is guided by the humanitarian principles affirmed by the UN General Assembly.

Humanity Neutrality Impartiality Independence

Coordinated

Planning alongside UN agencies and partners so efforts complement one another on the ground.

Accountable

Working through established humanitarian channels that put people, safety and transparency first.

People-centred

Every decision starts with the needs, dignity and rights of the people we serve.

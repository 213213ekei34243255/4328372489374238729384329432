Building the browser the web deserves.

Jonah started with a simple frustration: browsers had gotten slower, noisier, and less honest about what they do with your data. We set out to build something different: fast by default, private by design, and smart enough to get out of your way.

Today, Jonah is used by people who just want the web to work the way it's supposed to: quickly, quietly, and on their own terms.
